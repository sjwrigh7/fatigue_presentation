Introduction

Piston Fatigue

Fatigue failure is the primary concern for failure of internal combustion engine (ICE) pistons due to cyclic thermal and mechanical loads

  • Combustion pressure
  • Heat softening
  • Thermal stresses

Piston Modeling

Computer models can be used

  • Computational fluid dynamics (CFD) for combustion heat transfer and pressure
  • Finite element analysis (FEA) of piston temperature and stress

Piston Modeling

Requires specification of necessary parameters
  • Temperature dependent material properites
  • Thermal boundary conditions
  • Material fatigue characteristics

Parameter Uncertainty

  • These parameters must be specified appropriately for the computer model results to be useful
  • Many of these parameters are not fully known

Model Calibration

With so many unknown parameters, we look to rigorous approaches to estimate them

Piston fatigue analysis

Constant loading amplitude

  1. Guida and Penta [17] and Liu et al. [18] used Bayesian analysis of fatigue data.
  2. Karolczuk and Stonski used a Gaussian process to achieve non-parametric estimation of $S$-$N$ curves [19]
  3. Liu and Lu used a survival analysis approach to model fatigue data [20]

Literature Review

Piston fatigue analysis

Constant loading amplitude

  1. Feng et al used a physics-informed neural network to estimate probabilistic $S$-$N$ curves [21]
  2. Chiachio et al. used a Markov chain framework to non-parametrically estimate fatigue damage and failure [22]

Literature Review

Piston fatigue analysis

Constant loading amplitude

  1. Frie et al. used Bayesian optimal experiment design to select constant amplitude test designs for a parametric model [23]

Literature Review

Piston fatigue analysis

Variable loading amplitude
